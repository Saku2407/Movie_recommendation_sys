Under the Hood

How the recommendation engine works

Three Approaches

01

Content-Based Filtering

Represents each movie as a TF-IDF vector of genres, director, and year. Computes cosine similarity between vectors to find films closest in "feature space". Works for any user — no ratings needed.

TF-IDFCosine similarityNo cold-start
02

User-Based Collaborative Filtering

Finds K most similar users via cosine similarity on the ratings matrix. Predicts unseen ratings as a weighted average of those users' scores. Captures "users like you" taste patterns.

Ratings matrixPearson / cosineKNN
03

SVD Matrix Factorisation

Decomposes the sparse user-item matrix R ≈ U × Σ × Vᵀ into latent user and item factors. Each factor represents a hidden "taste axis". Predictions come from the dot product of user and item vectors.

Latent factors k=50SGD optimisationRegularisation
